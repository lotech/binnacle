  • Gone from the full dash: the centre car scene, the odometer tile and the CarPlay button (CarPlay is already on screen). Doors and the door animation return in the full dash; in the strip an open door would show as an orange line under the tyres.
  • Steering wheel: the top 64 px stay clear for the wheel rim, as on the full dash. Scroll moves through Camera / Apps / Full dash; OK opens.
  • CarPlay side: CarPlay on the left is the default. The real app gets a CarPlay side setting (Left / Right) that swaps the two.
  • How it would run: the unit's own "Screen display mode" (Right / Left / Full) already switches ZLink between a 1280 px split on either side and full width. In split mode the other 640 px is the system navigation-bar area. Our app would need to fill that strip, which is still to be tested on the unit.
